How is this different from a remote monitoring dashboard?

A dashboard shows everything and leaves the reading to your staff. MedPull checks every patient's wearable data and check-ins against their own baseline and the expected recovery curve, then gives you a short list grouped by risk, with the reason written out.

Which procedures do you support?

Total knee and hip replacement, ACL reconstruction, meniscus repair, rotator cuff repair, lumbar decompression, and ankle fracture ORIF, plus a general care pathway. Each has its own expected recovery curve.

Do patients need an app?

No. Patients can answer check-ins by text or through a link. The optional iPhone app adds Apple Health syncing and voice check-ins.

Which wearables work?

Apple Health (through the iPhone app), Oura, Fitbit, Garmin, WHOOP, Withings, Polar, and Dexcom. MedPull uses steps, walking speed and symmetry, heart rate, HRV, sleep, skin temperature, blood oxygen, and breathing rate.

Does it connect to our EMR?

Not yet. EMR integration is on our roadmap. Tell us which EMR you use and we'll keep you posted.

Does the AI make clinical decisions?

No. Scores and risk tiers come from fixed clinical rules. The AI writes summaries and message drafts for your team to review. Everything is for clinician review, not a diagnosis.

Is patient data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and wearable data comes through Junction, a HIPAA-ready health data platform. Before any real patient data is shared, we'll walk your team through HIPAA and BAA requirements.

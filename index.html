Check-ins that listen, not a script

MedPull knows the procedure, the day of recovery, and what the patient said last time. When a pain score jumps three points, it asks the follow-up a nurse would.

  • Two minutes, not twenty. Short enough that patients keep answering through week twelve.
  • Speaks up when it matters. Concerning answers go to the right clinician, transcript attached.

RTM revenue, minus the paperwork

Remote Therapeutic Monitoring is reimbursable, but tracking data days and minutes by hand usually isn't worth it. MedPull counts both automatically, from work your team already does.

  • Thresholds tracked live. See who has reached 16 days of data and 20 minutes, and who is close.
  • Documentation attached. Each code exports with the check-ins and clinician time behind it.
  • Built-in guardrails. A code only shows as ready once its requirements are met.

RTM code availability and reimbursement vary by payer and setting. MedPull supports your billing workflow and does not provide billing or legal advice.

Talk with us
How is this different from a remote monitoring dashboard?

A dashboard shows you everything and leaves interpretation to your staff. MedPull reads every check-in, compares it to the expected recovery path for that procedure, and produces a short ranked list of patients who need attention, with the reasoning written out. The goal is fewer things to look at, not more.

Which procedures do you support?

We're starting with the highest-volume orthopedic procedures: total knee and hip arthroplasty, ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, and meniscectomy. Each has its own recovery curve and check-in logic. Tell us what you do most and we'll prioritize it.

Do patients need to download an app?

No. Check-ins arrive as a text message and take under two minutes. There's nothing to download, no portal password, and they're available in multiple languages. That's why response rates hold up through week twelve.

How does RTM billing work?

MedPull automatically tracks the two things RTM codes hinge on: days of monitoring data and interactive treatment-management minutes. When a patient crosses a threshold, the code shows as ready with supporting documentation attached. Code availability and reimbursement vary by payer and setting, and MedPull supports your billing workflow rather than providing billing advice.

How does it work with wearables and our EMR?

MedPull connects directly to your EMR: patient and procedure data flow in automatically, and AI recovery summaries post back to the chart as progress notes. On the patient side, we integrate with wearables so activity, step count, and sleep join the same recovery timeline as their check-in answers. Tell us which EMR you run and we'll walk through the setup.

Is patient data private?

Patient data is processed securely, and enterprise plans offer HIPAA-aligned options and BAAs. Clinicians stay in the loop on every escalation. MedPull surfaces and summarizes. It does not diagnose or make clinical decisions.
